# Multiplayer



Workspaces in a Cloud organization are shared with the team. Members can open
the same workspaces and chats, see who's active, and continue each other's
work.

## Find your team's work [#find-your-teams-work]

Choose **Home** in the sidebar to see the organization's workspaces. Home
starts with your work and provides filters for:

* **By**: choose a teammate or **Anyone**.
* **In**: choose one or more projects.
* **Archived**: show or hide archived workspaces.

The sidebar keeps your **Pinned** and **My workspaces** sections close at
hand. Choose a person under **Team** to open Home filtered to their
workspaces. Presence dots show teammates who are currently active.

## Work in a shared chat [#work-in-a-shared-chat]

Open a teammate's workspace from Home or the sidebar, then select one of its
chats. The transcript and new agent output update live for everyone. When
another person has the workspace open, their avatar appears on the workspace,
and Conductor shows when they are typing in the same chat.

Create another chat when you want a separate agent thread against the same
workspace files, without creating another workspace.

## Follow a workspace [#follow-a-workspace]

Following is useful when someone else is assigned to the workspace but you
want to keep it in your workflow.

Choose the eye button in the workspace top bar, or right-click the workspace
in the sidebar or Home and choose **Follow**. The workspace appears under
**Following** in your sidebar. (You can't follow a workspace assigned to you —
it's already under **My workspaces**.)

A workspace's avatar stack shows its creator and followers, highlighted when
they currently have the workspace open.

## Hand off a workspace [#hand-off-a-workspace]

Right-click the workspace and choose **Reassign to** to make a teammate
responsible for it. The workspace moves into their assigned work; followers
keep following.

## Share a direct link [#share-a-direct-link]

Choose the link button in the workspace top bar, press `⌘⇧C`, or right-click
the workspace and choose **Copy link**. The link opens the workspace in
Conductor for any member of the organization.
